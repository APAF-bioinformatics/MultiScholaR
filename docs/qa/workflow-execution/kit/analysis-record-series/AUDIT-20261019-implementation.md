# Analysis-record series: post-hoc audit of the implementation (2026-10-19)

Mode: `post_hoc`, audit only. Scope: the delivered artefacts of KIT-025, 026, 027, 028, 029 and 033 at `1cfb78c3` (series closed on the DIA path), judged against two standards the series was created for (user, 2026-10-14: "every single parameter that gets used gets captured in a way that can be easily supplied alongside the user citing MS"; "a centrepiece of the research article"):

1. **Reproduction:** can a third party re-run the analysis from the record and the named inputs and obtain the same results?
2. **Journal submission:** does the rendered output state what a JPR methods section and its Supporting Information must state (software and versions, database identity, every parameter and threshold, the model and contrasts, data identifiers), in words a reviewer can read?

Evidence examined: the real journey's record (`kit-028/analysis_record-origin-screen.json`, 16 steps, 97 parameters), its DOCX and workbook (`kit-029/`), the DIA-NN report rendered from it (`kit-033/DIANN_report-with-record.docx`, `report-check-screen.json`), the replay check (`kit-028/replay-check-screen.json`), the six outcome files, the two earlier post-hoc audits and their remediations, and the code (`R/utils_workflow_analysis_record.R`, `inst/reports/shared/analysis_record_SI.rmd`, the registry). Nothing was executed; evidence layers are `implementation_conformance` (code read against the tickets) and `semantic_or_construct_validity` (the artefacts read against the two standards). The replay result is taken at its own layer (`runtime_behavior`, one host, one build) and not promoted.

## Verdict

The mechanism works and is proven at its stated scope: a record derived from the store replays 16 of 16 states hash-identically on the same build and host, inputs are hash-bound, every parameter is written in full, and the reports no longer print values that were never used. That is a real advance over `study_parameters.txt`, and the series' own audits closed the defects they found.

Measured against the two standards, it is **not yet submittable** and **not yet a complete reproduction record**. Three classes of gap: (a) one implementation defect that misrepresents three steps in every real record (IM-01); (b) the record stops at MultiScholaR's boundary, so the things a journal asks for first, database identity, upstream search provenance and data accessions, are absent (IM-04, IM-05); (c) the human-readable outputs are written for the kit, not for a reviewer, and the existing report still prints version fields as `NA` and fixed interpretive prose (IM-06, IM-07, IM-08). None of these contradicts the tickets' acceptance criteria as written, which is the point: the criteria proved the mechanism, not the endpoint.

Counts: 3 high, 8 medium, 5 low. Blocking for the paper's reproducibility claim as currently drafted: IM-01, IM-04, IM-05, IM-06.
