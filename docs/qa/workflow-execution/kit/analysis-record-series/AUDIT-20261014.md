# Analysis-record series audit (KIT-025, 026, 027, 028, 029, 033)

Mode: `audit_only`. Date: 2026-10-14. Scope: the six ticket files in `tickets/kit/` (untracked, as all KIT tickets), their plan rows in `00-KIT-PLAN.md`, and the code they cite on `performance-v2` (worktree head `6d28dbab`; dirty baseline: 22 modified files in `R/` and `tests/` from KIT-024, untouched). Endpoints audited against: (a) a JSON record MultiScholaR can replay; (b) a DOCX + Excel supplement for publication; (c) existing reports reading the record.

Evidence layers used: `documentation_or_graph_integrity` (inventory script, exit 0; its three "missing link" findings are the `[[state]]`/`[[sha]]` index notation inside backticks, false positives) and `implementation_conformance` (every file:line claim re-read in the code by two independent read-only passes, spot-checked by the auditor). No runtime, scientific or utility claim is made; nothing was executed.

Verdict: the series design is sound (derive from the store; provenance on the head so oracles stay fixed; replay as the completeness proof), but **four findings block implementation as written** (AR-01, AR-05, AR-06, AR-07) and the tickets need a revision 1.1 before any ticket starts. Counts: 4 high, 9 medium, 6 low.

## What holds

- Derivation on demand is supported by the code: `readDIAStateMetadata` reads records without `materializeDIAStateRecord`; `slots$args`, `config`, `timestamp`, `description`, `previous_state` are present per record; the head survives export/open in full (`dia_sessions.R:237, 265-268, 551-614`), so `file_dependencies` and `external_resources` round-trip.
- Record invariance: `kitFingerprintState` (`workflow_kit.R:904-926`) hashes record class/slots/dimensions/config only; head additions do not change it. `kitFingerprintStore` (:1103-1112) also records `head_sha256`, which **will** change with head additions: members that compare `head_sha256` must treat it as expected to differ (note for KIT-025's outcome).
- `createWorkflowArgsFromConfig` already receives `workflow_data$state_manager` (writer `:36-38, :90`), so KIT-033 item 1 is feasible without signature change; `ReadStudyParamsNew` and the `%||%` sites are where the tickets say.
- `jsonlite`, `openxlsx`, `rmarkdown` are Imports; the write pattern cited at `dia_sessions.R:307` is exact.
- The IQ constants and the `parameters = character()` entry are as cited (`operations.R:297-302`, `prot_declarations.R:84, 385-395`).
- `writer-r-preflight` is a runner selector (`run_workflow_checkpoint_proof.sh:223/348`), not a script; the ticket's use is consistent with kit-002.
