Mode: `audit_only` (user: "audit the ticket series for scientific rigor, completeness, implementation and reference correctness and best practices"). Nothing audited was edited; no run was launched. Worktree clean at the start.

Method: the mechanical inventory script (exit 2; its four "missing link" items are the R `[[...]]` index notation inside backticks, false positives as in the two earlier audits), then three independent read-only verification passes (A: KIT-038/039/020/042/043/021; B: KIT-049/046/047/041/053 and the script map; C: KIT-044/048/045, the stubs, the graph, the plan, the protocol and the conventions), each tracing every function, file, test, config key, line reference and behavioural claim to the code, and a scientific-validity layer by the auditor (iq's missing-value convention, limpa 1.4.0's `dpcDE`, `dpcQuant`, `dpc` and its RNG use, the RUV replicate structure, the decision design of the counted members). Ticket status is workflow metadata, not evidence.

## Verdict

The direction holds: every pending ticket is one bounded item on the kit's existing entry points, the graph is acyclic and consistent (one plan-row mismatch), the ledger matches every budget, and the Limpa route's science is implementable on the kernels named (limpa's `dpcDE` accepts a general design and a replicate block through its dots; `dpc` subsets deterministically; limpa draws no random numbers outside its simulators; iq treats `0` as missing by default, which is what the IQ writer emits for a skipped imputation). **None of the fourteen tickets can be started as written**: each carries at least one acceptance criterion that would fail against the code, a file list that misses a file its own items change, or a reference that points at the wrong function. Fourteen findings are high. KIT-038, the next ticket, needs a revision before it starts (A-01, A-02, A-03). Three decisions are the user's before KIT-021: a protocol v1.5 amendment for protein-level and non-DIA members, the LFQ pair's reading at a size that does not exceed the namespace floor, and the RUV replicate structure the `DIA_LIMPA` route declares.

Counts: 14 high, 49 medium (two of them medium-high), 34 low, 1 informational (98 rows; pass A 28, B 32, C 26, S 12). Blocked states: implementation 67, documentation 21, decision 6, execution 2, promotion 1.
