## Record fixes after the interim audit (AUDIT-KIT-262-m1m2-20261127; records only, no run, no script or number changed)

**M01 (caveat, owner decides).** The generator sets the 2 % floor values (cells set to 1) **before** the missingness mask (`m2-k262.R`, marked line [D], declared before any run), whereas KIT-172 and KIT-190 mask first and then set 2 % of the *observed* values to 1; the ticket says "2 % values at 1 as KIT-172". The consequence was not stated in the declaration. It has no effect with no missingness or under MCAR. Under abundance-dependent (AD) missingness a floor value sits at y = 0 and is missing with probability plogis(lod) = 0.208 at AD 20 % and 0.018 at AD 10 %, an MCAR-like overlay of about 0.4 % of cells at 20 % that falls on high-abundance features that would otherwise be complete. The auditor's deterministic integral (INFERRED) gives the expected complete-feature share under KIT-172's order against M2's order against the observed mean: AD 20 %, n = 24: 0.655, 0.592, 0.589; n = 48: 0.628, 0.514, 0.511; n = 96: 0.601, 0.404, 0.403; AD 10 %, n = 96: 0.702, 0.679, 0.676.
