**The declared rule, stated for the owner (a change to a scoring rule, made blind before any counted data).** Effect cells: if the standard deviation of a cell's paired FDP differences is below 10^-14, the differences are treated as equal and the cell is decided by the rule already declared for a standard deviation of exactly zero (KIT-190 `decide.R`, S4 (c)): no test statistic; "no worse" if and only if their mean is below the margin 0.05. Reason: FDPs are ratios of counts with denominators of at most 515, so two different paired differences differ by at least 1.4 x 10^-11, which gives a standard deviation of at least about 4.5 x 10^-13 even at R = 1,000 with a single differing data set; the stored rows carry 15 significant digits, so representation error is about 10^-15 at most; a standard deviation below 10^-14 can only be representation error. Under such noise Johnson's skewness correction (used when |skewness| > 1) divides by a vanishing standard deviation and can return "no worse" for differences far above the margin (on the constructed input every difference was 0.2, the standard deviation 2 x 10^-17 and Johnson's t -4.5 x 10^32). The change cannot alter a cell whose differences genuinely vary. (A property of the declared Johnson rule, not changed: with genuine but small spread and strong negative skewness its third term can push toward NW; the sign-flip p beside it makes any disagreement visible.)

**The guard's limit, in the auditor's words (to be repeated in the counted declaration).** The partial-match guard covers the scripts' own code, and package or dependency code that is not under a warning-muffling wrapper. A partial match inside `suppressWarnings`, or inside a handler that muffles warnings (6 uses of `suppressWarnings` in `utils_workflow_omics_declarations.R`; the M2-form helpers `m2Prepare` and `m2Sweep`), is honoured silently. No warning-catching handler exists in the route files (a grep for `warning = function` in 12 route files found none; dependencies not checked), so the option cannot divert the product's path there. The limit does not matter for what M3 claims: every field M3 compares is read with `[[` (exact match) or from trace columns outside any muffling wrapper, and a partial match inside the product under a muffling wrapper is the shipped behaviour and acts on both arms alike.
