# KIT-262 re-measurement of the abundance-dependent cells: counted declaration (committed alone, before any counted request)

**Purpose (VAL-381, option (b)).** M2's generator set the 2 % floor values before the missingness mask; KIT-172's order (which the ticket says the generator copies) sets them on observed values after it. This run measures the abundance-dependent (AD) cells again with the generator the ticket specified (KIT-172's order, `K262_FLOOR_ORDER=after`), as a separately declared run. **Both versions are reported; M2's version is not discarded and nothing in it is re-run.** The coordinator widened VAL-381's "about 16 cells x 40 data sets, 3 to 6 core-hours" to **all 88 AD cells of the 198 (3,520 data sets)**, because every AD sentence carries the caveat, including those saying no gain was seen (n = 6, n = 24), and a subset chosen where the first version showed a gain would be selected on the result; the owner is told. The decision (measure again in KIT-172's order, report both versions) is unchanged. M3's decision and its stress cell (cell 79, MCAR, VAL-381) are fixed and do not depend on this run.

**What is compared (descriptive).** Per cell, both orders side by side (M2's committed version and this one): complete-feature share (mean complete features and share), P, C1 and C2 without-RUV counts, C1-not-P, C2-not-P, C2-not-C1, the cause of P's failures (rescued by C1, completeness only), changed choice among P-with-RUV, realised and expected missing share. **The gain rule exactly as declared for M2** (lower Wilson limit at z = 3.452 of C1-not-P, C2-not-P, C2-not-C1 at least 0.05, 7 of 40) is applied by the unchanged scorer to this version's cells, with the exact-binomial counts at the same nominal level (9 of 40) reported beside it as description. **No new decision rule.** The M3 decision, the stress cell and the skip verdict are not reopened by this run.

**Predictions (for the checks only).** In every job: E1, E1', E2, E3, E4, E7 pass in all data sets (E1' in the r = 7 and 27 sets), E8 passes where run, COMPLETION equals the planned data sets, SEEDS and ORDERCOL pass in all rows, 0 error rows. No prediction is made for the figures.

**Jobs and timeouts.** Four jobs, one request each, balanced by M2's counted seconds of the same cells (`rm-plan.tsv`: 17,680 CPU-seconds each, planned 73.7 minutes at four workers, i.e. about 25 % of the 300-minute limit, below the 75 % bound); `timeout_minutes` 147 each (2 x the plan; M2's runner spread was 0.85 to 1.93 times the lifecycle's seconds). Script: `m2-k262.R <out>`, `K262_FLOOR_ORDER=after`, `K262_SEED_BASE=263100000`, `K262_CELLS` the job's cells, `K262_REPS` 1:40, four workers.

**Continuation rule (as M2's).** A job that does not complete is "did not complete" for its unwritten cells (never a denominator; cells are written after each batch, so no cell is partial); those cells are continued once, declared before it is sent, inside the remaining reserve; no completed cell is re-run. An unfavourable valid result is never re-run. A failed check with a mechanics failure and no result judged is an instrument defect under VAL-54 (at most three further runs).
