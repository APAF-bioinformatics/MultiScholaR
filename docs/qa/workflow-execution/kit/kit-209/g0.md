**The candidate refuses DA on the DIA `screen` design (U4, U5).** `no biological replication: every group is one
biological unit measured more than once (groups treated, contrast, control)`
(`R/func_prot_norm_optimization_helpers.R:426`), added by KIT-128 (40ae84bd, 2026-11-17, "a design whose every group is
one biological unit is refused in every omic"). The `screen` corpus's design gives every sample replicate 1, so the
bound technical-replicate assignment (the kit driver's and kit-118's) makes each group one biological unit. The base
runs DA on it. This is a product difference (class package error on inputs the base handled), not an instrument
fault, and not a gate-B failure (B3 concerns the base). Consequence: on U4 and U5 at screen size the candidate's DA and
report steps cannot be measured as bound. A decision for the coordinator before M1: measure U4 and U5 at screen without
those two steps on the candidate, or change the screen design binding (both builds), or another route.
