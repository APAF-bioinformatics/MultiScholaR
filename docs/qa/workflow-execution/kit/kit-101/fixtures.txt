2. Generator reproduction (F-06). `Rscript tests/testdata/e2e/generate_fixtures.R` writes:
   - the generated lanes: the five proteomics lanes, and trx_screen through the KIT-040 tool;
   - the retained prot_lfq/ and prot_tmt/ files, through their original writers, kept verbatim.
   It then checks every sha256 the manifest records: 51 files, all match. It stops with an error on any mismatch.

   Two consecutive runs gave byte-identical output (sha256sum over every fixture file), and `git status` showed no
   change to any committed lipid, metab, trx, prot_lfq or prot_tmt file.

   Not reproduced, kept, and recorded with the reason:
   - lipid_canonical (3 LipidSearch tables and the design): hand-made tables with integer intensities. The earlier
     writer's output from the same recipe differs, so the writer is removed.
   - metab_lc, metab_gc, metab_combined: hand-edited tables and hand-written MS-DIAL seed_*.csv examples. No writer
     exists for the custom import format.
   - prot_dia/seed_report.tsv, prot_dia_limpa/seed_report.tsv, prot_tmt/seed_proteins.tsv: hand-made. No reader.
   The entry-point oracles of the lipid and metab lanes are frozen on these bytes, so the bytes stay. The manifest
   records the reasons in each lane's fixture_recipe$hand_made and in retained_fixtures$note.
