# KIT-124 audit, 2026-11-16 (audit only; no repair applied)

**Question (user):** "are you confident in this ticket to bring us up to best practices and the best possible outcome? audit the ticket."

**Verdict: not as written.** The ticket would tell us whether our own candidate designs are calibrated on our own simulators. ## What the three sensitivity results already say (exploratory)

- Correct-then-test inflates false discoveries even with no unwanted factor at all (0.09 to 0.17 against 0.05).
- With the patterns in the model, every control rule is calibrated when there is no unwanted factor (0.045 to 0.057). The control-set bias appears only when an unwanted factor exists.
- RUV costs power when there is nothing to remove.

## Limits of this audit

- Same author and auditor. An independent statistical read of the repaired rule and arms would be worth having before W3.
- The audit judges the ticket's design. It does not establish that any RUV design is calibrated; only the repaired W3 can.
- Whether RUV stays on by default for any omic is an outcome of the measurement, not something a better ticket can promise.
