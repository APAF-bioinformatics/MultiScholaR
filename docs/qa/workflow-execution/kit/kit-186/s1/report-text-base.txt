 Transcriptomics analysis for kit097 - report - created on <TIME> Your_fancy_self <DATE> Methods Filtering and normalisation Genes were filtered with edgeR’s filterByExpr on the TMM-scaled library sizes of the design’s groups, and genes with zero variance across the samples were removed (300 genes before filtering, 188 after filterByExpr, 187 retained). TMM normalisation factors were computed with edgeR and log2 counts per million (prior count 2) were derived for the figures. Unwanted variation (RUVs) Unwanted variation was estimated with RUVs (RUVSeq) on inferred empirical negative control genes: the least significant genes of a preliminary group-only quasi-likelihood GLM, taken at a chosen percentage of the filtered genes. They are inferred from the data, not known in advance. The selection mode was automatic; the grid tested 5 to 30 % in steps of 5 %, with k capped at 3 and a k penalty weight of 0.05, choosing the maximum composite score. The chosen values were k = 2, 10 % of the genes, giving 18 inferred empirical negative controls, with a separation score of 0.9892 and a composite score of 0.9645. The RUVs factors W_1..W_k were added to the design and used as model covariates in the differential expression model; the counts themselves were not corrected. The RUVs-corrected logCPM was used for display only (PCA and RLE figures). Differential expression Differential expression was tested with the edgeR quasi-likelihood F-test: dispersions were estimated with estimateDisp (robust = TRUE), the model was fitted with glmQLFit (robust = TRUE), and each contrast was tested with glmQLFTest on that one fit. The realised model formula was ~ 0 + group + W_1 + W_2. P-values were adjusted with the Benjamini-Hochberg method, and genes with a false discovery rate (FDR, BH) below 0.05 were called significant. No fold-change threshold was tested; fold-change lines in the figures are visual guides. Integration export (RUVIII) No RUVIII integration export was recorded for this analysis.
