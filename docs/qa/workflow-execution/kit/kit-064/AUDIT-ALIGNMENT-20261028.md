## Touchpoints the audit did not see

- **The kit's exactness contract.** Repairs inside QC, normalisation, DA or annotation kernels change state fingerprints if they change any value; the series rule is that kernels are not rewritten and every change to a kernel's output is proven exact by the recorders and the journeys. Hygiene repairs that cannot change a value (`seq_len`, `vapply`, `drop = FALSE`, `&&`, `all_of`) are allowed inside kernels only with that proof attached (the KIT-028 replay, the four screen journeys, the full-corpus member).
- **No journey reaches enrichment.** The kit's GUI journeys end at the DA step; BUG-07 and BUG-08 live in the enrichment module, which no recorded journey, oracle or replay exercises. That is why they survived: the module contract tests drive the registration helpers with fakes that bypass promise semantics. The closure runner (KIT-065) extends the journeys through enrichment and the report.
- **Stage 3 owns the lipidomics and metabolomics modules.** Their `assign(.GlobalEnv)`, in-memory `getState()` observers and plotting helpers are rewritten onto the kit by KIT-030/031; repairing them twice is waste. They are listed here and carried to those tickets.
- **Platform.** The kit is x86_64 Linux (the P14 engine, cgroups, the fork-based fixture worker); Windows findings are recorded, not prioritised (KIT-051/052 are the portability gates).
- **Recorded decisions that look like hazards:** the `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1` pin (KIT-057, bit-identical fastlimpadpc), `set.seed(42)` in the enrichment helpers (reproducible ranks), `options(multischolar.test_mode)` in `run_app`, the `testthat` calls in `utils_workflow_execution.R` (the kit's fixture and fault handlers, test-only modes), the `fastduckdb` calls behind the engine probe (`diaRollupNativeInfo`, KIT-044's capability contracts).
