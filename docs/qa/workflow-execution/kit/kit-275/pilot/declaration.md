# KIT-275 revision 2.1 PILOT: declaration, committed alone, after the scripts, before any request (ticket R2.3, R2.5; audit RS2, P-1 to P-3)

**Nature.** The pilot is **unblinded and non-counted**. It reads nothing but the planted sample's best within-group correlation, one fixed genuine sample's, and the failure counts of the arm without RUV (NO) at the correlation filter's threshold 0.5, read before RUV, under the shipped normalisation. **It fixes only the acceptance of the positive control** (V1a, else V1b, else back to the owner) **and the expectation recorded for the counted PC1**; it sets no threshold (the counted falsifier is the single floor of 7.1 and 7.2). It runs no RUV journey and no fold change (the DA is stubbed), and it touches no counted row: **r1's tier D (`results/`) stays sealed and unread** (never opened, summarised or scored). Nothing here changes a margin, a level, R, an arm or the outcome table.

## 5. The rule (R2.3, fixed before the pilot) and the incomplete-cell rule (audit P-1)

- **Accept V1a if it is failed (best correlation below 0.5) in at least 45 of 50 data sets in every design.** If not, accept V1b by the same rule. If neither, **stop: no counted request is sent and the matter returns to the owner**; no other candidate is tried, no further pilot is run, the rule is not changed, the pilot is not re-run (instrument defects only, VAL-54). The pilot's choice replaces "V1a" everywhere in the ticket.
- **P-1: a cell with fewer than 50 valid journeys is an instrument defect, not a rejection.** A journey that is not `ok` is neither failed nor passed. The scorer's rule line then reads "incomplete" and the outcome is "no decision"; the affected units are re-sent once under VAL-54's four conditions (no value, comparison or rule changes; recorded in `runs.tsv`; the ceiling unchanged; at most three further instrument sends). The rule is applied only to complete cells; a complete, accepted V1a decides whatever V1b holds. Unit rows (`anomaly` = `unit`) are never denominators.
- **No acceptance count is predicted.** For information only, the plan read's linear stand-in (the regression of each sample's deviation on the row mean; it reproduces the diagnostic's replaced-profile medians as 0.38, 0.44, 0.50 against 0.44, 0.45, 0.55) gives the planted sample's best correlation after cyclic loess of about 0.26, 0.27, 0.30 at s = 4 and 0.08, 0.08, 0.12 at s = 6 (3, 4, 6 per group), against genuine pairs near 0.80: **indicative only, not a prediction and not a threshold**; the measurement can contradict it, and the rule above is the only decision.
- **P-3:** the pilot's timings (and its set-up, its unit seconds and its wall time) are **not used for G2, any counted timeout or any ceiling**; G2 uses L1-r2's timings only.
- The D3 genuine-sample count (below 0.5) is printed beside each D3 line (non-specificity at 3 per group, R2.4); it enters no rule.
- What the pilot can not show: acceptance of a control is not a claim about how common such deviations are; 50 data sets per cell (a two-sided 95 % interval about +-0.14 wide in the middle, narrower at the ends); one genuine comparator sample.
