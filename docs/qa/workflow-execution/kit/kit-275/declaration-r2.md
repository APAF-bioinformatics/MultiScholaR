## 4. The order

1. **Tier D's twelve jobs** (the `D-*` rows of section 3), then every tier D continuation, if any (section 5). Nothing else is sent before tier D is complete.
2. **The interim controls read**, once, only after all tier D continuations: `score-k275.R <tier D rows dir> <out>` with `K275_CONTROLS_ONLY=1` only (budget row 30 job-minutes: limit 20 + 10 set-up). It writes `controls-judged.tsv` (PC1 and NC1, with the fixed genuine sample's count below 0.5 beside each PC1) and prints only the six labels with those counts; **no outcome row, no tier D cell, no "outcome" line**; it reads V1a (the deciding version) for PC1 and V0 on GK2 for NC1, and never V1w. **Readings:** a **pass** is exit 0 with all six labels `passes`. **F1 or F2 is read as exit 1 AND `controls-judged.tsv` present AND a `FAILS` label** (PC1 failing at the single floor, fewer than 144 of 200 in a design; or NC1): then every other tier's request is cancelled, the owner is told (the numbers of `controls-judged.tsv` go to the owner) and nothing else is read. **A coverage stop (S-3) is exit 1 AND `controls-judged.tsv` present AND a `not determined (coverage)` label and NO `FAILS` label** (PC1 has fewer than 0.90 R valid V1a journeys, as when a tier D data set is missing or the V1a rows are absent): it is **not F1**; it is a coverage stop: hand back, nothing else is sent until the coordinator's word, and the missing units are found by the procedure of section 5. **Exit 1 without `controls-judged.tsv` is an instrument stop**: handed back, nothing else sent.
3. **Then the remaining tiers**, in this order: K (6 jobs), F+S (6), V (6), M (1), ID (1), T (3), E (3), each with its continuations as needed.
4. **The deciding scoring, once**, over the 38 jobs and their continuations in one directory (each job directory under its own name, which is its source; tier E's comparators come from tier K; crossNO needs D and E), `K275_G1=docs/qa/workflow-execution/kit/kit-275/l1r2/out/scripts/2/out/g1.tsv` (the re-run G1's table, committed with the L1-r2 outputs; the path is checked before sending), `K275_BOOT_B` at its default 10,000, `K275_SCORE_R` at its default 200, 100, 20 and `K275_SCORE_BASE` at its default 2000; a job that has `checks-<job>.tsv` contributes it, not its `progress/` files. Timeout 30, ceiling 40 job-minutes. Tier E rows with status `refused` (not `refused_after_exclusion`) are counted and handed back (each is an instrument defect, R-1).
5. **Procedural rule: no tier D row is opened or summarised before the final scoring**, beyond the continuation line counts of section 5 and the interim controls read of step 2. The primary outcome is not seen early. Tier V is reported only (its numbers never decide the outcome row); an identity failure among tier V's journeys (E1's mask clauses, E5) stops tier V's part and is reported to the owner; it does not reopen the deciding outcome.

## 5. The continuation rule (verbatim) and the missing-unit procedure

**Verbatim (the coordinator's ruling, VAL-382):** a timed-out job's missing data sets are run ONCE; an interrupted data set is discarded whole; no partial re-run of a cell; a data set complete in two sources stops scoring; one continuation per job, inside the reserve and under the running-ceiling rule.
- **Missing units, found without reading any value.** A unit `<tier>-<design>-<r>` is missing when `rows/part-<tier>-<design>-<r>.tsv` is absent from the job's directory (a job that finished has its parts assembled into `rows/<tier>-<design>-<first>-<last>.tsv.gz`) or holds fewer data lines (line count minus the header) than its tier's rows: **D 17, K 17, F 5, S 4, V 12, M 7, T 6, E 6, ID 5**. Only line counts are used.
- **The continuation:** the original's tiers, design, first and last, with `K275_ONLY_UNITS=<the missing tags, comma-separated>` (tags outside the job stop the run), the job name gains `-cont`; its own out directory (`k275-<job>-cont`); `timeout_minutes` = ceil(1.5 x its units' compute); its ceiling is taken from the reserve and written in `runs.tsv` first. **One continuation per job**; a continuation that itself times out is not repeated: its complete units count, the rest is reported to the coordinator.
- **Scoring input for a timed-out job:** the part files of the killed job directory stay in the records and are scored as their own source (an incomplete data set is dropped whole and listed as "interrupted"). The scorer stops on a data set complete in two sources, on a unit row, on a false `unit_completed` check, on an instrument row and on duplicate or out-of-range rows (RANGE-1: r outside 2001 to 2000 + R of its tier).
- **Pre-scoring line-count check of the checks (S-4; counts only):** per complete tier D data set **8 E1 and 1 E2 check rows, i.e. 4,800 E1 and 600 E2 over the 600 tier D data sets** (200 per design; 400 E1 and 50 E2 per tier D job of 50 data sets); the reported (not E1, not F4) checks `R1_dev_mean` and `R1_dev_sd` are 1 row per data set each (600 each). A shortfall is a missing or interrupted unit, handled by the procedure above before scoring. A data set whose rows are complete but whose E1/E2 rows are missing is reported, not re-run.
