### Root causes of rows 10, 11, 14, 15 and 17 (2026-11-16, user asked why)

Evidence: `kit-114/sim/ruv_why.R` and `ruv_why.txt` (toy data), `kit-114/sim/ruv_offsets_in_store.R` (937q), the code named below.

- **Rows 10, 11 and 14 are one inherited problem: the classic proteomics RUV correction path** (DIA with IQ, LFQ, TMT). It predates the KIT series, and the series kept its numbers bit-identical, so exactness could not show it. No journey but the DIA full one runs it (the LFQ and TMT fixture journeys skip RUV; DIA_LIMPA uses covariates). DIA_LIMPA's path, written in KIT-041, has none of the three faults.
  - **Row 11 (FDR 0.22 to 0.48).** With no technical replicates the replicate structure is the group (`resolveProtRuvReplicateBlocks`), so RUV-III removes k of the n - g within-group dimensions, and the DA then estimates its variance from the corrected values with n - g degrees of freedom (no adjustment anywhere). Toy check: variance kept 0.16 to 0.27 at n = 6, k = 3 (t statistics about 2 times too large); 0.75 at n = 12; 0.92 at n = 24.
  - **Row 10 (M2).** The same mechanism. The fewer usable controls, the less the correction removes (toy: variance kept 0.75, 0.89, 1.20 with 44, 12, 6 controls at n = 12). Complete-case controls give RUV more usable controls, so it removes more and the FDR rises. M2 did not fail because complete controls are worse controls.
  - **Row 14 (offsets, k = 44).** Two faults. (1) `findBestNegCtrlPercentage` takes k from `findBestKElbow` with no upper limit; `max_acceptable_k` (2 to 5 by sample size) only enters the score that ranks percentages. DIA_LIMPA's `findBestNegCtrlPercentageCapped` limits k. (2) `RUVIII_C_Varying` is given uncentred log2 values, so the controls' mean level (about 22) enters W and each protein is shifted by a constant. Toy check: median shift 1.2 log2 at k = 2 with 200 controls, 31 to 36 at k = 44 with 50 controls; the same call on protein-centred values with the means restored has no shift and identical contrasts (difference under 1e-7). On 937q: corrected values from -105 to 270, median shift 27 log2, correlation of protein means before and after 0.016.
- **Row 15 (M1 memory).** The rule was put in the reader, on the data frame the import holds in the app. The two quantity columns were lazy (`vroom`) and are now rebuilt in memory (5.85 million rows, 94 MiB). The import is the DIA route's one step that is not on disk: it already peaks at 2.30 GiB before M1.
- **Row 17 (composite).** Proteomics publishes through a journaled transaction run by the controller (`publishWorkflowActionOutputs`); the composite is written inside it. KIT-122's E1 was scoped as "QC figures" without first measuring which figure cost the memory, and the stage figures were moved. In progress: KIT-122 revision 1.1.
