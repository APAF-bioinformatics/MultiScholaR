# KIT-017 post-hoc audit (2026-10-16, audit only): why the closure pair was rejected

Mode `post_hoc`, against protocol v1.4 as frozen and the ticket's revision 1.1. Scope: stage `kit-journey-017` (the counted rerun, both members complete and verified; decision `rejected`, `step_not_worse`: DA step 1.129 ×, whole slice 1.077 ×, resident end 0.927 ×), the mechanism it measured (KIT-019, DA publication through the worker) and the records that led to it (KIT-012, KIT-019, the closure's own predeclaration). Findings before any repair; no artefact of the stage is changed. The question asked: why the pair did not win.

## Evidence gathered for this audit

- Per-step anonymous peaks and the coordinator's memory trace at 50 ms for both members, read at the DA step (`<proof>/kit/runtime/kit-journey-017/{control-1,candidate-1}/memory-trace.tsv`, `journey-phases.tsv`).

- The worker's log: `glimmaXY` called at 22:38:47 with 2,476 proteins × 297 samples; the slice spike at 22:38:55.

- A direct measurement, not a guarded run: `pandoc treated_vs_control.html -f html -t html --standalone --embed-resources` on this host (pandoc 3.11) peaks at **3,123 MiB resident** and exits after a few seconds.

## What this audit does not establish

The size of the transient on other corpora (it scales with the page, 14.7 MB here for 2,476 proteins × 297 samples); the behaviour of pandoc on other hosts; whether the acknowledgement defect also affects the peptide and protein QC managed steps (their completion records were not read for this audit).

States: `execution_complete` (four counted members, four screen runs); `decision_supported` (the pair's rejection and the stage claim stand as measured; the rejection's cause is now named); `promotion_authorized` none; `claim_scope` the synthetic 300-sample DIA corpus on this host.
