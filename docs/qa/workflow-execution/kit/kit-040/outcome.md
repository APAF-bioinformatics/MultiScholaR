## Limits

- The references are synthetic regression inputs with known truth; they establish nothing about RUV control or k selection on real data.
- Products are those of the ticket's table plus what the product map needs (`count_matrix_filtered`, `df_summary`, `fit_final`, the whole `qlf`, `ruvs_counts`, `ruviii_M`, `ruviii_ctl_mask`, `top_up`/`top_down`, the RLE plot data, y-limits and recomputed whiskers, the PCA axis labels). Derived products (effective library sizes, `filterByExpr`'s minimum sample size and median library size, PCA variance fractions from the plot scores, RLE whiskers) are labelled derived. The renderers' figures other than the PCA and RLE plots (CCA panels, tuning curve, volcano, bar plot) are not frozen as `layer_data()`; their files are hashed as recorded-only. KIT-093's renderer parity needs those added here first (next action).
- KIT-093 rows with no notebook object (`control_source`, `method`, `m_source`, `m_label`) are in the map as labels or literals, not reference products.
- The `transcriptomics` worktree `../MultiScholaR-transcriptomics` (branch `transcriptomics` at `e7db197f`, no commits of its own, an ancestor of `performance-v2`) is left as it is for the user.
- Rollback: `git revert` of this ticket's commits; the gitignored TRX files are restored from the tracked JSON; the kit library rollback is removing the fifteen packages the receipt lists from that overlay (and from any overlay installed from it).
