## 3. Size rule (written before the first point)

- Raw genes are held at 60,000, the all-biotype end of real gene tables (GENCODE about 62,000; protein-coding about 20,000).
- **Low-count fraction 0.6:** 60% of the genes get a base mean in [0.05, 1], the corpus recipe's low range, so `filterByExpr` drops them. The rest keep the KIT-040 log-normal base mean (meanlog 4, sdlog 1.5), about 13% of which also fall below the 10-count cut. The expected retention is about 35% (about 21,000 genes), a typical all-biotype retention. The recorded retention is the measured one.
- **Samples are stepped.** The sizes:
  - **small:** 24 samples (`trx_60k_024`);
  - **medium:** 100 samples (`trx_60k_100`);
  - **large:** 200 samples (`trx_60k_200`), the sample count of KIT-084's lipid and metab large, so the omics share a sample axis;
  - **ultra:** below.
- **If small, medium or large does not complete inside the limits, the work stops and is reported.** Sizes are never reduced to get a pass; changing them is the user's decision.
- **The size table** records per size: raw genes, retained genes, samples, raw cells, retained cells, corpus key, recipe, seed and manifest sha256. It compares them with the two-assay lipid and metab cells on KIT-088's axis (KIT-084 large: 2 assays x 20,000 features x 200 samples = 8.0 M cells; screen 9,600 and 14,400).

## 4. Ultra rule

Ultra is the largest trial point above large (400, 600 or 800 samples) that completes inside the limits. The first point above it that does not finish is recorded as the limit, with the step it stopped in and the peak it reached. If no point above large completes, ultra equals large and the 400 point is the limit; that is reported, and the user decides.

## 7. Decision rules (verbatim) and terminal outcomes

- **Exactness:** every member must be exact against its own build's in-process oracle (protocol v1.8 section 10.1). Kernels are never rewritten. Memory is reported. Time is reported and never judged.
- **Journeys:** `kitSingleJourneyDecision`, with the six listed worker steps each needing a completed worker journal (10.5) and every history state judged by name.
- **Pairs:** `kitOperationDecision` (`tools/profiling/workflow_kit.R`), recorded verbatim. The rule as the code applies it:
  - **Status:** `invalid` if any member fails an eligibility rule (`kitIneligible`, the verifier included). Otherwise `rejected` unless every pair is not worse. Otherwise `accepted`.
  - **Not worse:** per pair, the candidate's kernel peak (`peak_bytes`) is at most 1.02 x the control's, and the candidate's whole-slice anonymous peak (`sampled_anon_bytes`) is at most 1.02 x the control's.
  - **Reduction:** the candidate's kernel peak is at most 0.95 x the control's. It is reported and not required.
  - **Promotion:** `promotion_authorized = FALSE`.
- **KIT-080's selection rule, shown for reference:** switch a step only on an accepted decision with a reduction. It was written for real data, and here it is applied to synthetic data; that divergence is stated with the pairs. The default is decided by the user in KIT-100.
- **Time ratios** carry the file-cache caveat: one run each, control first.
- **Filter pre-pass (F07):** the bound declaration's pre-pass is the TMM-effective library size, as in the notebook (:166-170). `trxPartitionedFilter` (`R/utils_workflow_trx_declarations.R`) computes `calcNormFactors(DGEList(counts, group), "TMM")` on every gene and uses `lib.size * norm.factors` for each page's `filterByExpr`. KIT-095's step map declares the same (`step-map-trx.json`, `trx_expression_filter`, `global_statistics`). So there is no stop on this point.
  - The pre-pass phase's peak and time are reported separately from the batched phase. The operation members record no phases for this operation (the harness records phases for `precursor_rollup` only), so they come from the call profile (6a).
- **Terminal outcomes:** a stop at a limit is a valid **not-finishing** result. The limits are:
  - the worker step deadline (1,800 s);
  - the slice early stop (15.5 GiB of the 16 GiB cap);
  - the member ceiling;
  - for a trial point, the in-process ceiling or step limit.

  Such a stop records the step and the peak reached, is never retried and has no finite ratio. It is not-finishing even though `kitIneligible` labels the member `invalid`.

## 8. Expected machine time

Estimated from the screen records (`trx98a`: 33 s in process; 098a: 188 s journey) and the kernels' scaling.
- edgeR's fits are about linear in genes x samples.
- The grid's `ruv_cancorplot` runs a `cancor` loop over k = 1..n twice per member, six members. That is about 12 n^4 flops on LINPACK QR, so about 15 to 30 s at 200 samples, 3 to 5 min at 400, 15 to 30 min at 600 and 40 to 80 min at 800. 800 may reach the 1,800 s step limit.
