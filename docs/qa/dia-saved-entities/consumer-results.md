## Required producer-consumer handoff

DIA-015 still requires the broader DIA-009 history qualification, the completed
DIA-013 revision 1.6 decision and appropriately bound DIA-014 export correctness,
plus accepted `G2_history:iq/standalone-qs2-large-history-v3`. The earlier v2
failure remains historical evidence. The three-step exception does not qualify
those broader gates or authorize GUI integration; no further run follows
automatically from this pilot closeout.

Before timing, name each real producer, immutable artifact, consumer request,
statistical population, natural reuse count, current resident/disk access and
required output. Five accesses to one table are not five QC generations.
Do not retain assays, force the resident control through RDS, or manufacture
reads to reproduce a favourable result. Measure projected-only consumers
separately when claiming projected-only peak; the existing bundle includes full,
projected and range reads.

Use one matched installed policy build and the existing runner. Include creation,
writes, computation, rendering, hits/misses/invalidation/eviction, full session
hydration/export, publication/copies and restore in the proper boundary. Report
working-memory overlap, native/file-cache memory and unattributed costs explicitly.
Arithmetic producer/read sums and unit-test counts are not composed acceptance.
Preserve scientific populations, `runTestsContrasts(weights = NA)`, S4/public
state semantics and complete self-contained RDS sessions.

The final GUI 10%-elapsed/10%-peak thresholds, uncertainty rules, immediate
comparator non-regression and 8 GiB/zero-swap/900-second/64 MiB limits are unchanged.
Even the repeated reader's peak upper ratio 0.900645 misses a strict <=0.90
threshold; it must not be called final performance acceptance. Limpa and other
omics inherit no IQ evidence.

  model or route.

## Gate decision

`G2:iq` is held because required prerequisite, large correctness/performance and
paired composition evidence are missing, while the valid observed treatments are
unfavorable for retained history, selective elapsed and safe-export elapsed.
This is not a claim that every large product consumer must regress; it is the
required refusal to extrapolate. DIA-016 science replay and DIA-018–023 GUI
integration remain unauthorized under the current ticket graph.

Rollback is the existing production path: none of the experimental stores/codecs
is GUI-active. Preserve all external receipts, request journals and saved records;
do not reset the branch or delete negative evidence. A future redesign requires a
new explicit decision on the failed history prerequisite and a matched large
measurement boundary, not a retry of unchanged cases.
