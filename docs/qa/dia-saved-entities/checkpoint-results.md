### Decision and remaining requirement

Disk-backed complete checkpoints are useful for **ordinary history residency**
on this recorded large synthetic DIA journey. qs2 is a justified candidate for
further checkpoint qualification. The actual R/S4 data and public native session
format are preserved; inactive states need not stay in R memory for persistence.

These are single pairs, with fresh processes but no claim of cold or identically
controlled OS caches. The two installed builds/comparison boundaries are kept
separate; qs2 is not compared to the older resident control to manufacture a GUI
gain. The replay restores recorded states separately and does not model all
allocation sharing during forward scientific execution. Runtime worker specs are
preserved independently; reactivation of every parallel scientific stage has not
been qualified by this storage experiment.

G2_history/G2/G3/G4 remain held: the frozen workload matrix, seven independent
pairs, uncertainty and complete GUI/scientific integration are not supplied by
this pilot. No GUI storage switch or broad architecture replacement was made.
The active-QC result and its separate measured optimization target are recorded
in measurement.md. All large raw receipts/profiles remain in their named
browser-smoke directories under the existing physical proof root.
